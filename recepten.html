How to make them

Breakfast & pre-workout

  • 1. Savoury oats with egg & spinach: Cook 50g oats in 150ml low-fat stock. At the last moment, stir through 100g fresh spinach until it wilts. Top with a poached egg and 15g pumpkin seeds.
  • 3. Matcha protein overnight oats: Mix 45g oats, 30g vegan vanilla protein powder, 1 tsp matcha and 10g chia seeds. Pour in 150ml almond milk, stir well and leave overnight in the fridge.
  • 7. Cottage cheese & avocado toast: Toast 2 slices of wholegrain or sourdough bread. Spread with 100g cottage cheese (Hüttenkäse) and layer over ½ a mashed avocado. Season with chilli flakes, pepper and salt.
  • 9. Chia-lupin pudding with blueberries: Mix 25g chia seeds and 15g lupin flour (or protein powder) with 150ml plant milk. Let it set for 4 hours. Top with 75g fresh blueberries and a pinch of cinnamon.
  • 10. High-protein pancakes: Blend 40g oat flour, 1 scoop (30g) whey or pea protein, 1 egg and 100ml skimmed milk into a smooth batter. Cook small pancakes in a pan with a little spray oil.

Lunch & dinner

  • 2. Vegan lentil-chickpea curry: Fry curry spices (turmeric, cumin) in a pan. Add 100g lentils and 100g chickpeas (both tinned) with 150g chopped tomatoes. Simmer for 10 minutes and stir through 50ml light coconut milk and a handful of kale.
  • 4. Salmon & quinoa clean bowl: Cook 60g quinoa. Pan-fry 125g fresh salmon fillet. Serve the quinoa and salmon in a bowl with steamed broccoli, 50g edamame and a dressing of a little lemon juice and olive oil.
  • 5. Tempeh & broccoli stir-fry: Cube 120g tempeh and fry it crisp in a wok. Add 150g broccoli florets and ½ a bell pepper. Season with a splash of low-salt tamari (soy sauce), ginger and garlic. Serve with 50g brown rice if you like.
  • 8. Chicken & sweet potato traybake: Cube 150g chicken breast and 200g sweet potato. Toss with 1 tbsp olive oil, paprika, salt and rosemary. Spread on a tray with 100g asparagus tips and bake 25 minutes at 200°C.

Functional snacks & shakes

  • 6. Ashwagandha recovery shake: Blend 1 frozen banana, 30g protein powder of choice, 250ml almond milk, 1 tsp ashwagandha powder (an adaptogen for stress and recovery) and 10g almond butter into a creamy shake. Perfect after an evening session.
