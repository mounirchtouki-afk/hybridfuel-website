FAQ

Good to know.

How is this different from a regular calorie tracker?+
A tracker makes you do the work: look everything up and log it. HybridFuelCo gives you a complete plan — with weights, shopping list, recipes and training — tuned to your training days. You follow and tick off, instead of puzzling it out yourself.
Who is HybridFuelCo for?+
For hybrid athletes — anyone who combines running and endurance work with strength training, from Hyrox competitors to people who just lift and run to stay fit. It works at any level and any age: a 25-year-old racing their first Hyrox just as much as a masters athlete training in their forties.
Do I have to do Hyrox to use it?+
No. HybridFuelCo is built around Hyrox-style training, but the plan, macro calculator and recipes work for any hybrid athlete — runners who lift, CrossFit, functional fitness, or general strength-and-conditioning.
Can I cancel monthly?+
Yes. You cancel in your account; you keep access until the end of your paid period. A yearly plan is 2 months cheaper than monthly.
Subscription or one-off — which do I pick?+
The subscription is the app you use daily: plan, recipes, training, tracking and race day. Prefer a one-off? Order a single week plan as a download — no account, no commitment.
Is my data safe?+
Your connection is always encrypted and your data is kept private to your own account; you can delete it yourself. See our privacy statement.
